01LoCoMo Benchmark

Same dataset.
Different architecture.

LoCoMo (Long-term Conversational Memory) is a benchmark for evaluating how well memory systems retain and retrieve information from long conversations. It's the benchmark mem0 chose for their published evaluation — we ran the same test.

* mem0 token figure from mem0.ai/blog (2024). † mem0 quality measured via LLM-as-judge on a 0–100 scale — not directly comparable to token-overlap F1. We report F1 because it's dataset-verifiable.

Key Finding

Prexi compresses 26.8× harder than mem0 while achieving a higher F1 score (0.079 vs full-context baseline of 0.069). Compression preserved more relevant signal than no compression at all.

What This Means

At 259 tokens vs mem0's 6,956 — if you're running 1M queries/month, Prexi saves ~6.7B tokens per month versus mem0's approach. At $0.50/1M input tokens, that's $3,350/month saved.

04Under the Hood

Three models.
No cloud dependencies.

Every model runs on CPU. No GPU required, no external embedding calls, no data leaving your stack.

Classify

DistilBERT

Fine-tuned on 10 conversation domains. Returns domain, intent, risk level, and emotional state. Runs in under 10ms on CPU.

Compress

T5

Sequence-to-sequence compression. Removes conversational noise while preserving semantic meaning. Domain-aware — medical conversations skip compression entirely.

Cache

MiniLM

384-dimensional semantic embeddings. Detects semantically equivalent queries before compression. Cache lookup under 5ms.
