01The Problem

Support threads are LLM cost traps.

A ticket that runs 15 turns sends the full transcript to the LLM on every reply. Turn 15 might be 4,000 tokens of context — 70% of which is greetings, re-explanations, and resolved sub-issues from earlier in the conversation.

You pay for all of it. The model's attention dilutes. Response quality quietly drops. And you have no visibility into why.

Without Prexi
  • Turn 1→ 120 tokens
  • Turn 5→ 780 tokens
  • Turn 10→ 2,100 tokens
  • Turn 15→ 3,940 tokens

Cost grows linearly with conversation length

02How It Works

Compress. Cache. Forward.

Classify the ticket

DistilBERT reads the conversation and detects domain (customer support), intent, sentiment, and escalation risk. Runs on CPU in <10ms. No external API call.

Compress the history

T5 summarises resolved earlier turns while preserving unresolved issues, account details, and escalation context. Average 60% reduction for support threads.

Cache repeated queries

MiniLM catches semantically identical questions ("where is my order?", "order status?", "track my package"). Cache hits skip LLM entirely. Savings compound across all users.
